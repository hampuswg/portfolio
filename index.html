About

I trained as an industrial design engineer at Luleå University of Technology, which means user research, prototyping, ergonomics and a lot of CAD. My thesis took me to Nairobi to work on a 3D-printed prosthetic arm, and I stayed on the project after graduating.

Since 2017 I've worked on the same family of training software, first at Vizendo and, after the acquisition, as product manager at Siemens. I know the people who use it: operators learning a new assembly sequence, and the engineers who build that training for them.

I'm good at turning messy input from customers into clear decisions, and at working between developers, sales and users. I'm now sharpening the craft side: interaction design, prototyping in Figma and usability testing.

Outside work: skiing, music, film, food and most sports.
